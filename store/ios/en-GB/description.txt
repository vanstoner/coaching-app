Grassroots football should be fun for every child, and fair. Heart of the Game: Coach makes one part of a coach's Saturday easier: knowing who comes off next, and whether everyone is getting a fair share of the game.

It is a substitution reminder for one squad. Fairness here means total time on the pitch, in goal and outfield together. Positions follow each child's strengths and your choices; the app counts the minutes.

BEFORE KICK-OFF
- Add your squad by first name, with each child's position preferences: who likes to go in goal, and where they like to play outfield.
- Set up your fixtures, or press Play now.
- Plan every quarter or half. The app shows each child's projected minutes, so you can see the fair share before a ball is kicked.

ON THE TOUCHLINE
- The match clock runs from the real time, so it stays right even if the phone goes to sleep in your pocket.
- When a planned sub is due, the phone buzzes, and the sub is on the screen.
- Drag players around the pitch, and record goals, saves and the score as they happen.

AFTER THE FINAL WHISTLE
- A short match report: the score, the scorers, saves, the subs made, who was missing, and each child's minutes against the match's fair share.
- Press End match and it counts towards the season.

ACROSS THE SEASON
Four views on the Squad screen, each showing its answer at a glance:
- Season grid: each child's minutes in each match.
- Fairness: how close everyone is to the squad average, and how that gap has changed match by match.
- Going into Saturday: who is owed time over the season.
- Positions tried: where each child has played. A record, not a target.

Each child also has their own page, with their minutes per game, matches played and missed, by competition.

FIRST NAMES ONLY, AND YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
You enter children's first names only. There is no account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter is kept on your phone. It is included in your phone's own iCloud backup, so a new phone can get the season back, and you can export a minutes file whenever you choose. Forget everything, in Settings, wipes it from the phone.

Free, for the coach, so every child gets the most out of the game.
