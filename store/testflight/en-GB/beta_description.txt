Heart of the Game: Coach helps a volunteer coach of a junior football team answer two questions at the touchline: who comes off next, and is every child getting a fair share of playing time? Plan each period's line-up and substitutions, run the match clock, and see each child's total minutes across the season. Everything stays on the phone: no account, no server.

Please try it with a made-up team (Getting started offers one) or your own squad, and tell Rob what was confusing, slow or wrong.
