No sign-in. The app opens empty: it holds only what a coach enters, on the phone.

To see a full season quickly (made-up names only):
1. In Safari on the test device, open https://github.com/vanstoner/coaching-app/raw/main/test-data/test-season.json and save it to Files.
2. In the app: Settings > Import minutes file, and choose test-season.json. It adds 10 players and a past season.
3. Open the Squad tab: the four tiles (Season grid, Fairness, Going into Saturday, Positions tried) and any child's page.

To try a match: Home > Play now, pick the starting seven, start the clock, tap a bench player to plan a substitution, and play to full time for the match report.

Settings > Forget everything empties the app again.

Data: everything stays on the device. First names only. No account, server, analytics, advertising or tracking. Privacy policy: https://vanstoner.github.io/coaching-app/privacy.html
