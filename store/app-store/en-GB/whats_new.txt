The biggest update since launch: subs on cue, every result recorded, and your season backed up.

SUBS ON CUE
• The clock now lists this quarter's subs with their times. Thirty seconds before each one it rises green, turns amber, then red if you're late, and the phone buzzes once. Tap Done and the sub is made.
• Planned subs start together at the half-period, and the plan you share with parents shows exact times.

EVERY MATCH, PROPERLY RECORDED
• Results on played matches ("W 4 – 1"), worked out from the goals. Add a goal afterwards even if you don't know the minute.
• Awards: Player of the Week, Mr Assister, or your own, with joint winners.
• Your own tallies (saves, tackles, anything you like), each with its own button during play.
• Each match shows how far it can be trusted: live and signed, or entered afterwards.

FAIR SHARES, AT A GLANCE
• The squad list is in order of minutes a game, and every table sorts by its numbers so gaps stand out.
• Fill-in players from another team get a fair share on the day without skewing your squad's figures.

5-A-SIDE AND 40-MINUTE MATCHES
• Two 5-a-side formations (1-2-1 and 2-2, with a keeper) beside the 7-a-side ones, and 40 minutes joins the match lengths.

YOUR SEASON, SAFE
• Back up the whole app to one file, and restore it on a new phone: squad, preferences, fixtures and every match.
• Importing onto a squad you've typed in offers to match children by name, so the same child isn't added twice.

EASIER TO START
• First open shows a new coach three steps, or try it with a made-up team.
• Test matches from Settings, run at up to ten times speed, never touch your real figures.

Thank you to every coach who tried it early. Your notes built this one.
