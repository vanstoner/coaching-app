Heart of the Game: Coach answers one question at the touchline: who comes off next, and is everyone getting a fair share?

It is made for grassroots and junior football coaches running small-sided matches in quarters or halves.

BEFORE THE MATCH
• Add your squad by first name.
• Add fixtures, or just press Play now.
• Plan every period: who starts, who comes off and when, with each child's projected minutes before kick-off.

DURING THE MATCH
• A match clock that keeps the right time even if the phone locks or the app is closed.
• The pitch view: drag a player to swap positions or to make a substitution.
• The phone buzzes when a planned substitution is due, while the app is open.
• Record goals, saves and goals conceded, and the score follows.

AFTER THE MATCH
• A match report with every child's time on the pitch, in goal and outfield.
• Season views: each child's minutes match by match, how close everyone is to the squad average, who is owed time going into the next match, and the positions each child has tried.

FAIR BY DESIGN
Fairness is total time on the pitch, in goal and outfield together. Positions are a record, never a target.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• First names only: no surnames, dates of birth, contact details or photos.
• Everything stays on your phone. No account, no server, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking.
• Included in your phone's own backup, held in your own Apple account. Export a minutes file whenever you choose.

Free, with no in-app purchases.
