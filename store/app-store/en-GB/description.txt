Who comes off next, and is everyone getting a fair share? Heart of the Game: Coach answers both at the touchline, for grassroots and junior football played in quarters or halves.

NEW IN 2.2.0
• 9- and 11-a-side formations, beside 5- and 7-a-side, and matches of 70 and 80 minutes.

NEW IN 1.3.0
• Sub alerts: 30 seconds before each planned sub, the clock's prompt rises green, turns amber, then red if you're late, and the phone buzzes once. Tap Done and the sub is made.
• The plan you share with parents shows every sub's exact time.
• Results from your goals, awards, and your own tallies on each match.
• 5-a-side formations and 40-minute matches, beside 7-a-side.
• Back up the whole app to one file and restore it on a new phone.

PLAN BEFORE KICK-OFF
• Build your squad by first name, with each child's position preferences and who likes to go in goal.
• Add fixtures, or just press Play now.
• Plan every period: the starting line-up, each substitution and when it happens, with every child's projected minutes shown before a ball is kicked.
• Suggested line-ups put on first the children who have had least time in the match so far, and share the goalkeeping round. The app suggests; you decide.

RUN THE MATCH
• A match clock that keeps true time even if the phone locks or the app is closed.
• This quarter's subs listed above the pitch, each with its time.
• Drag and drop players on the pitch to swap positions or make a substitution.
• Plans change: move a sub, change who comes off, or re-plan the rest of the match in a few taps.
• Record goals, saves and goals conceded, and the score keeps itself. Slipped? Undo.

SEE THE SEASON
• A match report at the final whistle: the result, every child's time on the pitch, in goal and outfield, against their own average.
• Squad views: everyone's minutes match by match, how close the squad is to an even share, who is next in line for more time going into the next match, and the positions each child has tried.
• The squad list in order of minutes a game, so gaps stand out.
• A page for every child: playing time, match by match, positions tried and preferences.
• Fill-in players from another team get a fair share on the day without skewing your squad's figures.

FAIR BY DESIGN
Fairness is total time on the pitch, in goal and outfield together. Positions are a record, never a target.

RECORDS YOU CAN TRUST
• Every minute is worked out from what happened in the match, so a total and its record can never disagree.
• Mistakes are fixed with a correction and a note, never by rewriting what happened.

EASY TO START
• First open shows three steps to be ready for Saturday, or try it with a made-up team.
• Test matches from Settings, at up to ten times speed, never touch your real figures.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• First names by default, with an initial to tell two children apart. No place for surnames, dates of birth, contact details or photos.
• Everything stays on your phone. No account, no server, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking.
• Included in your phone's own backup, held in your own Apple account. Back up to one file whenever you choose.

Free, with no in-app purchases.
