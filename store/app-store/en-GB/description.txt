Heart of the Game: Coach is the substitutions planner, match-day assistant and season tracker for grassroots and junior football. Plan who plays when, run the match from the touchline, and know that every child is getting a fair share of time on the pitch.

Made for small-sided matches played in quarters or halves.

PLAN BEFORE KICK-OFF
• Build your squad by first name, with each child's position preferences and who likes to go in goal.
• Add fixtures, or just press Play now.
• Plan every period: the starting line-up, each substitution and when it happens, with every child's projected minutes shown before a ball is kicked.
• Suggested line-ups put the children who are owed time on first and share the goalkeeping round. The app suggests; you decide.

RUN THE MATCH
• A match clock that keeps true time even if the phone locks or the app is closed.
• Drag and drop players on the pitch to swap positions or make a substitution.
• While the app is open, the phone buzzes when a planned substitution is due, so nobody is left on the bench.
• Plans change: re-plan the rest of the match in a few taps.
• Record goals, saves and goals conceded, and the score keeps itself. Slipped? Undo.

SEE THE SEASON
• A match report at the final whistle: every child's time on the pitch, in goal and outfield, against their own average.
• Match analysis: this match's minutes for each child, set against the season so far.
• Squad views: everyone's minutes match by match, how close the squad is to an even share, who is owed time going into the next match, and the positions each child has tried.
• A page for every child: playing time, match by match, positions tried and preferences.

FAIR BY DESIGN
Fairness is total time on the pitch, in goal and outfield together. Positions are a record, never a target.

RECORDS YOU CAN TRUST
• Every minute is worked out from what happened in the match, so a total and its record can never disagree.
• Mistakes are fixed with a correction and a note, never by rewriting what happened.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• First names only: no surnames, dates of birth, contact details or photos.
• Everything stays on your phone. No account, no server, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking.
• Included in your phone's own backup, held in your own Apple account. Export a minutes file whenever you choose.

Free, with no in-app purchases.
