# Try Heart FC Coach

Heart FC Coach helps you run a junior match from the touchline. It reminds you
who comes off next, and keeps everyone's minutes fair across the season. It's
free, it has no adverts, and nothing you put in it leaves your phone unless you
send it.

On an iPhone it's in the **App Store**. On Android it's still being tested
in the Google Play Store, so Rob adds you first, and anything you tell him
shapes what comes next.

**Which phone do you have?**

- [iPhone](#on-an-iphone)
- [Android (Samsung, Google Pixel, Motorola and most others)](#on-an-android-phone)

## On an iPhone

[![Download on the App Store](https://vanstoner.github.io/coaching-app/app-store-badge.svg)](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/heart-of-the-game-coach/id6819699395)

1. Tap the badge above on your iPhone, or search the App Store for
   **Heart of the Game: Coach**.
2. Tap **Get**. It's free, with no adverts and no in-app purchases.
3. The app appears on your home screen as **Heart FC Coach**.

**Good to know**

- Updates arrive through the App Store like any other app. Your squad and
  matches stay as they are.
- Had the TestFlight version? Install from the App Store and everything you
  entered is kept. You can then delete TestFlight.

## On an Android phone

You'll get it from the **Google Play Store**, the same as any app. Because
it's a test version, Google needs Rob to add you first.

1. **Send Rob the email address you use on your phone.** That's your Google
   (usually Gmail) account. To find it, open the **Play Store**, then tap your
   picture or initial in the top right; the email is shown there.
2. Rob adds you and posts a **"Join the test" link** in the WhatsApp group.
   Open it **on your phone**.
3. Tap **Become a tester** (or **Join**), then the link to **download it on
   Google Play**.
4. On the Play Store page, tap **Install**. The app appears as
   **Heart FC Coach**.

**Good to know**

- **Please keep it installed for at least two weeks.** Google only lets an
  app go public once enough people have tested it for 14 days, and you're
  helping it get there.
- Updates arrive through the Play Store like any other app.
- If the Play Store says the app isn't available, the link was probably
  opened before Rob added you, or under a different Google account. Check the
  email you sent matches the one in the Play Store, then try the link again.

## Getting started (both phones)

1. Open the app and tap **Add your team**: its name, the season, the age
   group and the format (5-, 7-, 9- or 11-a-side). Want a look first? **Try
   it with a made-up team** fills every screen with a made-up squad.
2. Add your squad on the **Squad** tab. **First names are best.** For two
   children with the same name, add an initial ("Alex S"). The app never asks
   for surnames, birthdays or photos, and warns before saving a longer name.
3. Add your next fixture with **+ Fixture**, or press **Play now** when you
   kick off. The app tells you who's due off.
4. After the match, tap **End match** at the bottom of the report. The
   **Squad** tab then shows everyone's minutes for the season.

**The [how-to guide](https://vanstoner.github.io/coaching-app/guide.html) walks through a whole week**, from planning
the match to seeing who is next in line for more time.

**Keep your season safe:** **Settings › Backup › Back up this app** puts every
team into one file you can save or email to yourself. If you change phones,
use **Restore from a backup** on the new one.

## Telling Rob what you think

Anything confusing, anything that went wrong at a match, anything you wish
it did: send it to Rob on WhatsApp. A screenshot helps a lot.

Your squad's details stay on your phone and are never sent to Rob, so
please don't post children's minutes or names in the group.
[How the app looks after your data](https://vanstoner.github.io/coaching-app/privacy.html).
