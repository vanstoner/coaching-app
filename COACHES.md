# Try Heart FC Coach

Heart FC Coach helps you run a junior match from the touchline. It reminds you
who comes off next, and keeps everyone's minutes fair across the season. It's
free, it has no adverts, and nothing you put in it leaves your phone unless you
send it.

You're getting it early, before it's in the app stores. That means you get new
versions first, and anything you tell Rob shapes what comes next.

**Which phone do you have?**

- [iPhone](#on-an-iphone)
- [Android (Samsung, Google Pixel, Motorola and most others)](#on-an-android-phone)

---

## On an iPhone

You'll use Apple's **TestFlight** app. It's Apple's own way of handing out apps
before they're in the App Store, and it's free.

1. On your iPhone, open the **link Rob posted in the WhatsApp group**. It
   starts with `testflight.apple.com`.
2. If you don't already have TestFlight, the page shows a button to get it
   from the App Store. Install **TestFlight**, then **go back to the WhatsApp
   message and tap the link again**.
3. TestFlight opens and shows **Heart of the Game: Coach**. Tap **Accept**,
   then **Install**.
4. The app appears on your home screen as **Heart FC Coach**. Open it from
   there, the same as any other app.

**Good to know**

- A small orange dot next to the name just means it's a test version.
- When Rob releases a new version, TestFlight tells you. Open TestFlight and
  tap **Update**. Your squad and matches stay as they are.
- Each test version stops working after 90 days, so keep it updated.
- When the app is in the App Store, you can install it from there and keep
  everything.

## On an Android phone

You'll get it from the **Google Play Store**, the same as any app. Because
it's a test version, Google needs Rob to add you first.

1. **Send Rob the email address you use on your phone.** That's your Google
   (usually Gmail) account. To find it, open the **Play Store**, then tap your
   picture or initial in the top right; the email is shown there.
2. Rob adds you and posts a **"Join the test" link** in the WhatsApp group.
   Open it **on your phone**.
3. Tap **Become a tester** (or **Join**), then the link to **download it on
   Google Play**.
4. On the Play Store page, tap **Install**. The app appears as
   **Heart FC Coach**.

**Good to know**

- **Please keep it installed for at least two weeks.** Google only lets an
  app go public once enough people have tested it for 14 days, and you're
  helping it get there.
- Updates arrive through the Play Store like any other app.
- If the Play Store says the app isn't available, the link was probably
  opened before Rob added you, or under a different Google account. Check the
  email you sent matches the one in the Play Store, then try the link again.

## Getting started (both phones)

1. Open the app and add your squad. **First names only.** The app never asks
   for surnames, birthdays or photos, and you shouldn't add them.
2. Add your next fixture, then press **Play now** when you kick off. The app
   tells you who's due off.
3. After the match, the **Played** list and the season tables show everyone's
   minutes.

**Keep your season safe:** **Settings › Back up this app** puts everything
into one file you can save or email to yourself. If you change phones, use
**Restore** on the new one.

## Telling Rob what you think

Anything confusing, anything that went wrong at a match, anything you wish
it did: send it to Rob on WhatsApp. A screenshot helps a lot. On an iPhone,
take a screenshot inside the app and TestFlight offers to send it straight to
him.

Your squad's details stay on your phone and are never sent to Rob, so
please don't post children's minutes or names in the group.
[How the app looks after your data](https://vanstoner.github.io/coaching-app/privacy.html).
