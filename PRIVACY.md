# Privacy policy: Heart of the Game: Coach

*Last updated 31 October 2026. Applies to the app named **Heart FC Coach** on the
phone and **Heart of the Game: Coach** in the App Store, on iPhone, iPad and
Android.*

This app helps a football coach plan substitutions and see that every child
gets a fair share of time on the pitch. It is designed so that the developer
never receives any information about you or your players.

## What the app holds

Only what the coach types in or records during a match:

- **Players' first names**, sometimes with an initial or a second word to
  tell two children with the same name apart. The app asks for first names
  and warns before saving anything longer, recommending an initial. It has
  no place for surnames, dates of birth, contact details or photos.
- **Match records:** who played, when, and in which position; time in goal
  and outfield; goals, assists, shots on target, saves and goals conceded;
  tackles, corners and free kicks won, and any tallies the coach adds; awards
  given; who was available for each match.
- **Teams, seasons, fixtures and settings:** each team's name, its seasons
  (a name, age group, level and match format), opponents' names, kick-off
  times, match length, the pitch's surface and condition, and preferences
  such as who prefers to play in goal.
- **The referee's name**, if the coach types one in for a fixture. It is
  shown only on the coach's own match screens: never in the minutes file or
  in a shared plan. It is in the app backup.
- **Notes the coach writes**, for example when correcting a record. Please
  keep children's personal details out of notes.
- **A signing key for this copy of the app.** The app makes it on the phone
  and keeps it in the phone's secure storage. It signs what the app records,
  so a record can later be shown to be unaltered. It identifies this
  installation of the app, not a person, and holds no information about
  anyone. Its public half is included in the minutes file and the app backup
  so the signatures can be checked; its private half never leaves the phone
  through the app.

## Where it is kept

**On the phone.** There is no account, no sign-in and no server. The app
sends nothing to the developer or to any third party.

## What the developer receives

**Nothing.** The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no
crash reporting.

## Backup

The released app is included in the phone's own backup: **iCloud** on an
iPhone or iPad, and **Google** backup on Android. That backup is held in the
coach's own Apple or Google account, under that account's security, so a
lost or replaced phone can get its season back. The developer cannot see it.
The coach can turn backup off in the phone's settings.

Test builds of the app ("Heart FC Beta Coach") are never included in a backup.

## Sharing

The app shares data only when the coach chooses to, in one of three ways:

- **Export minutes file**, a copy of one team's records.
- **Back up this app**, one file holding everything the app keeps, for every
  team: the squads' first names, fixtures, plans, settings and the records.
  It is for moving to a new phone or keeping a safe copy. **Restore from a
  backup** shows what a file holds before anything changes; what was on the
  phone is kept aside on the phone, not deleted.
- **Share plan**, a picture of one match's plan for parents: the starting
  positions, the planned substitutions and the bench, with children's first
  names. It shows no minutes and does not say who is unavailable. The app
  makes the picture only for that share and does not keep it.

Where a file or the picture goes, and who sees it, is then the coach's
choice. Keep files holding children's names somewhere private.

## Deleting data

**Settings › Danger zone › Forget everything** removes the squad, its matches, every
recorded minute and any copies the app kept aside (after a restore, or when
saved data could not be read) from the phone, for every team.
Removing a single team sets it aside on the phone, where it can be brought
back; it is not deleted. It keeps the signing key, which
holds no information about anyone. Uninstalling the app does the same. A phone backup made before then
can keep a copy until Apple or Google replaces it with a newer backup.

## Children

The app is for the coach, an adult. It is not designed for children to use.
It holds children's first names and match records only because the coach
enters them, and keeps them on the coach's own phone.
