Fair playing time,
without the spreadsheet.

The substitutions planner, match-day assistant and season tracker for grassroots and junior football. Plan who plays when, run the match from the touchline, and know every child is getting a fair share.

How to use it

See it in action

Every picture uses a made-up squad. New to the app? Read the how-to guide.

Plan before kick-off

Know every child's minutes before a ball is kicked.

Your teams and seasons

One team or several, each with its own squad, age group and seasons. Start a new season and the squad comes with you.

5-, 7-, 9- or 11-a-side

The formations for your format, in quarters or halves, from 40 to 90 minutes. The age group suggests the size; you choose.

Every period planned

The starting line-up, each substitution and when it happens, with projected minutes for everyone. Share it with parents as one picture.

Suggested line-ups

The children who have had least time go on first, and the goalkeeping is shared round. Or just press Play now. The app suggests; you decide.

Run the match

One hand, one phone, a game going on.

A clock you can trust

Keeps true time even if the phone locks or the app is closed.

Sub alerts

30 seconds before each planned sub the prompt rises, green, then amber, then red if you're late, with a buzz. Tap Done and the sub is made.

Drag and drop

Move players on the pitch to swap positions or bring someone on. Plans change: move a sub or re-plan the rest of the match in a few taps.

Goals, assists and the score

Record them as they happen, with your own tallies and awards; the score keeps itself. Slipped? Undo.

See the season

Is everyone getting a fair share? The answer, at a glance.

Match report

The result, and every child's time on the pitch, in goal and outfield, against the match's fair share.

Squad views

Minutes match by match, how close the squad is to an even share, who is next in line for more time, and the positions each child has tried.

Why the difference

Tap a child to see the sum behind their figure and every match it comes from. Every figure explains how it is worked out.

A page for every child

Playing time, match by match, positions and preferences. Fill-ins from another team get a fair share without skewing your figures.

Fair by design

Fairness is total time on the pitch, in goal and outfield together. Positions are a record, never a target.

Records you can trust

Every minute is worked out from what happened in the match. Mistakes are fixed with a correction and a note, never by rewriting history.

Private by design

First names only. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no server, no analytics, no adverts, no tracking. Privacy policy.
