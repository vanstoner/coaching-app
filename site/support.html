Support

Found a problem, or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Please never include a child's surname, photo or any other personal details.

Common questions

Where is my data?

On your phone, and in your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google). Nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

I have a new phone. How do I keep my season?

Restore your phone from its backup, or on the old phone use Settings › Export minutes file, then Settings › Import minutes file on the new one.

How do I start again?

Settings › Forget everything removes the squad, its matches and every recorded minute from the phone.

Does the clock keep going if I lock the phone?

Yes. Time is worked out from the moment each period started, so a locked phone or a closed app loses nothing. The buzz for a substitution comes while the app is open.

How does it decide what is fair?

Total time on the pitch, in goal and outfield together. Suggested line-ups put on first the children who are owed time; you always decide.
