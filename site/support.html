Common questions

Where is my data?

On your phone, and in your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google). Nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

I have a new phone. How do I keep my season?

On the old phone use Settings › Backup › Back up this app and save the file somewhere private. On the new phone, tap Restore from a backup on the first screen (or in Settings › Backup). That brings everything, for every team: squad, fixtures, plans, settings and every recorded minute. Restoring the whole phone from its own backup (iCloud or Google) works too.

How do I start again?

Settings › Danger zone › Forget everything removes every team, its squad, its matches, every recorded minute and any copies the app kept aside from the phone. Back up the app first if you might want them again.

Does the clock keep going if I lock the phone?

Yes. Time is worked out from the moment each period started, so a locked phone or a closed app loses nothing. The buzz for a substitution comes while the app is open.

How does it decide what is fair?

Total time on the pitch, in goal and outfield together. Suggested line-ups put on first the children who have had least time; you always decide. Tap the ⓘ beside any figure, or see Settings › Player minutes › About the figures, for exactly how each one is worked out.

Why doesn't a match show in the season figures?

A match counts once it is closed with End match at the bottom of its report. A match left out, voided, or in another format than the season's is not counted either; its report says so.

I coach more than one team.

Tap the team name at the top of Home, then + Add a team. Each team keeps its own squad, fixtures and figures.

How do I start a new season?

Settings › Season › Start a new season. The squad carries over, the age group moves up a year and every season figure starts at zero. Past seasons stay readable.
