For testers: the test season

A made-up squad and a past season of matches, so you can try every screen without typing anything in. Every name in it is made up; no real child is in it.

Download the test season

On an iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Download the test season.
  2. Safari asks whether to download test-season.json. Tap Download. It goes into the Downloads folder of the Files app.
  3. Open Heart FC Coach and go to Settings › Import minutes file.
  4. The file picker opens. test-season.json is usually at the top of Recents. If it isn't there, tap Browse › Downloads. Tap the file.
  5. The app says what it imported. The made-up squad and its past matches are now in the app, and Settings shows each child's minutes.

On an Android phone

  1. Tap Download the test season. It saves to Downloads.
  2. In the app, go to Settings › Import minutes file and pick test-season.json from Downloads.

Good to know

  • It imports only into an app that hasn't recorded matches of its own. On a phone holding a real season, the import is refused and nothing changes.
  • When you've finished, Settings › Forget everything removes it all.
